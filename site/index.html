How we measure

  • Denominator is contacts who finished the sequence and were Declined, plus everyone at Response Received or better.
  • Mid-sequence contacts (Contact Made) are left out until they reply or finish.
  • Any human reply, including redirects and "not interested", goes to Response Received before Declined.
  • Hard bounces go straight to Declined. HubSpot doesn't flag them separately, so they count here unless removed.
  • Email health: ~30% opens on the full cadence, 50–125 words, and the only ask is a 15-minute discovery call. Never cold-send a deck.
